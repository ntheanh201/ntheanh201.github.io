Outstanding IT Individual across Viettel, 2025

I was honored as one of the Outstanding Individuals of Viettel’s global Information Technology line for 2025 (Tôn vinh cá nhân xuất sắc Ngành dọc Công nghệ thông tin Viettel toàn cầu năm 2025). The recognition was presented at the Group’s IT conference for the second quarter of 2026.

It carries my name, but almost everything behind it was teamwork: a year of building the AI and GPU platform together, and learning from the open source communities we build on. Thank you to my teammates, and to everyone who put my name forward. I’m grateful, and I’ll try to keep earning it.
