CNCF published the 2026-2028 Ambassador cohort

CNCF just published the 2026-2028 Ambassador cohort, and my name is on the list 🎉

58 new ambassadors this year, selected from more than 600 applications, joining a cohort of 312 worldwide. I’m the only one from Vietnam in this intake 🇻🇳

The post names translating the Cloud Native Glossary as one of the ways ambassadors give back. That’s where my path started, with the Vietnamese edition, and it’s the kind of work I want to keep doing: bringing more Vietnamese engineers from user to contributor.

Thank you Audra Montenegro and the CNCF team.
