![VIETTELDX TALKS #8 poster: AI is knocking, are Vietnamese businesses ready?](/img/talks/2025-08-14/poster.jpg)

This one was a talk show, not a talk: no slides, three guests, in Vietnamese. VIETTELDX TALKS is a series
by Viettel Solutions, and episode #8, "AI gõ cửa: Doanh nghiệp Việt đã sẵn sàng?" ("AI is knocking: are
Vietnamese businesses ready?"), was released on 14 August 2025. You can watch it on
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8pN4ft0OzpY) or on the
[Viettel Solutions website](https://solutions.viettel.vn/vi/vietteldx-talks/vietteldx-talks-8.html).

The premise is that a business doesn't need to be big before it adopts AI; it needs to take the right first
step. I was one of three guests, alongside Nguyễn Quang Tuấn (AI and Data Analytics Expert, Viettel
Solutions) and Đặng Văn Đức (Business Development Director, Intel); I joined as Cloud Platform Expert,
Viettel Cloud. Below are the
points the show put on screen for my part.

![Nguyễn Thế Anh, Cloud Platform Expert, Viettel Cloud, on VIETTELDX TALKS #8](/img/talks/2025-08-14/frame-lower-third.jpg)

**Start early.** AI is no longer a question of "whether", but of "deploying as early as possible".

![AI is no longer "whether", but "deploy as early as possible"](/img/talks/2025-08-14/frame-quote-adopt-early.jpg)

**More than compute.** Viettel Cloud provides more than raw compute: a complete AI ecosystem that is
secure, efficient, operated and supported by an experienced team.

**People.** To close the AI talent gap and use the government's policy framework, a business can assess its
real needs and current staff, upskill its team, and use the ecosystem and policies around it.

![Three steps to close the AI talent gap](/img/talks/2025-08-14/frame-quote-ai-talent.jpg)

**Security.** A business urgently needs its own AI platform, to reduce the risk of losing information
security.

![A business urgently needs an AI platform to reduce information security risks](/img/talks/2025-08-14/frame-quote-ai-platform.jpg)
