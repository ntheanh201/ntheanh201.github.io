Hi, I'm The Anh Nguyen (**@ntheanh201**), based in Hanoi, Vietnam. I lead a team building GPU infrastructure
and LLM serving on Kubernetes, and I speak at community events about what we learn along the way.

## Now

I'm the **Solutions Architect for the Viettel AI Platform** at **Viettel Networks**, a multi-tenant GPU supercomputing platform, and I lead the **Token Factory** project.

- **Token Factory:** LLM inference at scale with NVIDIA Dynamo, serving multiple LLM families
- **GPU fleet:** NVIDIA H200 clusters on NVIDIA AI Enterprise and Base Command Manager (BCM)
- **GPU and fabric:** GPU Operator and Network Operator, InfiniBand with GPUDirect RDMA, multi-node NCCL, and HAMi fractional-GPU sharing for multi-tenancy
- **Upstream:** contributing to NVIDIA DSX OS components: KAI Scheduler, Run:ai, Workload Map, AI Cluster Runtime (AICR) and Cluster Readiness Engine (NVCRE)
