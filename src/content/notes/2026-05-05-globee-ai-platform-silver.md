---
title: "Viettel AI Platform: Silver at the 2026 Globee Awards for Artificial Intelligence"
date: 2026-05-05
---

The Viettel AI Platform, which our team at Viettel Networks builds and runs, won Silver (Best of Category) at the [2026 Globee Awards for Artificial Intelligence](https://globeeawards.com/2026-winners-artificial-intelligence-awards/), in the category GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) & Compute Orchestration.

![Silver Globee certificate for the Viettel AI Platform, 2026 Globee Awards for Artificial Intelligence](/notes/2026-05-05-globee-certificate.jpg)

The entry was about GPU-as-a-Service and compute orchestration: one self-service platform for GPU servers on bare
metal or VMs, an H200 cluster with InfiniBand and GPUDirect RDMA for distributed training, Kubernetes clusters, and
notebooks that share GPUs in fractional slices. It all runs on a Kubernetes control plane that passed CNCF's
[Kubernetes AI Conformance](https://github.com/cncf/k8s-ai-conformance/tree/main/v1.35/viettel-ai-platform), which is
where the "Certified Kubernetes" in the citation comes from. One dashboard tracks utilisation across the GPU fleet, so
idle cards can be found and given back.

![The team behind the Viettel AI Platform](/notes/2026-05-05-globee-team.jpg)

This is the team's award: the engineers who built the platform and keep it running, and the open source communities behind Kubernetes, HAMi and the other CNCF projects it stands on. Thank you all.
